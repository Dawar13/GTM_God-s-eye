# Research log

Every research action that adds, changes or rejects a fact gets one row. Conflicts between sources are recorded here (PROJECT_CONTEXT §5.6): keep both sources on the record, pick the primary one, and say why in the "conflicts" column.

- **date**: ISO date the research was done
- **entity**: record id (`inv_…`, `fund_…`, `co_…`, `rnd_…`) or a plain name if no record exists yet
- **question**: what we were trying to establish
- **sources checked**: URLs, including ones that turned up nothing
- **result**: what was recorded (or `null` and why)
- **conflicts**: disagreements between sources and which one won
