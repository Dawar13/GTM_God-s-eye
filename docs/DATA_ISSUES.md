# Data issues found in Phase 2

Data is frozen in Phase 2. These are logged, not fixed. Each line: what, how many, where it shows.

- 2026-10-31 (S2): 2,553 of 3,317 investors have `region: null`, so the Western default filter shows 709 and "Western share" is computed over the 764 with a region (92.8% Western). Any region comparison undercounts the unknown group.
- 2026-10-31 (S2): 2,569 of 3,317 investors have no freshness (no fund on record), so "fresh funds" and fresh dry powder cover only the 748 investors with a dated fund.
- 2026-10-31 (S2): 87 of 1,210 funds have no close date or vintage year (not on Funds closed by year); 90 have no size (left out of dollar sums).
- 2026-10-31 (S2): 1 fresh Western investor has a latest fund with no size, so Fresh dry powder is a lower bound by that one fund.
- 2026-10-31 (S2): The top of "Top 10 by fit score" is held by investors flagged `sparse` (only two or three of six features known, rescaled to about 100). The score is correct per `src/lib/fit/score.ts`; the flag is shown, but sparse rows outrank well-evidenced ones. Handled in the view: the tile ranks non-sparse investors only (DECISIONS, S2).
- 2026-10-31 (S2): Only 118 of 1,034 non-competitor companies have a `layer`, so "our layers" covers 118 companies plus 44 competitors; rounds of other companies are not in the Recent deals chart.
- 2026-10-31 (S6): Thesis coordinates are sparse. 558 of 3,317 investors have a thesis-map position (`layout_thesis`, those with a thesis vector); the Western default shows 262 and leaves 447 out ("missing thesis vector"). Stage earliness exists only for the 204 investors with `stages`, 191 of them on the map, so the Fit view shows at most 191 (170 Western; 92 Western map investors lack earliness).
- 2026-10-31 (S6): Capital bubbles need a check size, and only 114 investors have one (103 with both bounds). 104 investors have all three axes (93 Western); 95 of the 104 have a latest fund size, and the other 9 are drawn as fixed-size rings.
- 2026-10-31 (S6): For 7 investors the pipeline's latest fund (`layout_bubbles.latest_fund_usd`, chosen by close date, then size) differs from the Directory's "Latest fund" (`latestFund`, chosen by vintage year, then close date): Boldstart, Crane, Daybreak, Eclipse, Haystack, Sempervirens and Sequoia. The bubble size and its tooltip follow the pipeline. The Directory and the S2 stats follow `latestFund`. Resolved in the view: bubbles now use the Directory's `latestFund` (DECISIONS, S6 review fix); the pipeline's `latest_fund_usd` still differs for these 7 and is unused by the app.
- 2026-10-31 (S6): UMAP left a few tight islands far from everyone else (x ≈ −7.8 and 23.8, y ≈ −3.8 and 8.4). Each holds more than 1% of the points. The map's first view leaves them out and states the count (20 of 262 Western, 32 of 558 overall); zooming out reaches them.
- 2026-10-31 (S6): The pipeline chose k = 9 thesis clusters, one more than the 8-hue data palette, so the ninth takes Tableau 10's next hue (`#FF9DA7`). See DECISIONS, S6.
- 2026-10-31 (S7): Graph investors are sparsely described. Of the 2,849 investors in graph.json, 2,439 have no `region` (so the Western default shows 384), 2,595 no `type` and 2,324 no thesis cluster; the network draws them in the neutral `#4A5160` and the legend counts them. Only 37 of 1,074 graph companies have `total_raised_usd`; the grey shade falls back to the sum of known round amounts (907, a lower bound) and 130 stay unknown.
